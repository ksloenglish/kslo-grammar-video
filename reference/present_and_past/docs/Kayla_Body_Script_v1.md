## Quiz answer key

| # | Question (on screen) | Answer | Why (tag on screen) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Look! The baby ________ (sleep). | is sleeping | Look! = right now (present continuous) |
| 2 | I ________ (do) my homework when the lights went out. | was doing | long action, interrupted (past continuous) |
| 3 | Right now, I ________ (want) a new phone. | want | non-action verb; *am wanting* is struck through |
| 4 | In the film, the hero ________ (fly) to the moon and saves the planet. | flies | synopsis = present; spelling chip *fly → flies* |

I added context to questions 3 and 4 so that only one answer fits. Without *Right now*, *wanted* would also be correct. Without *and saves the planet*, *flew* would also be acceptable in a past-tense retelling.

## Changes from the textbook pages

| Change | Reason |
|---|---|
| *I was born in 2011* → *I was born in 2013* | Matches current students' birth years (as in the approved plan) |
| Left out: *We often walk our dog in the morning.*, *My grandpa passed away two years ago.*, *The cake smelt great.* | Time; one example per idea. The bereavement example also sits awkwardly with a cheerful host |
| Fairy-tale passage cut after *…who had a kind heart* | Time; *was* and *had* are enough to show past tenses in a story |
| Shark passage cut after *…they spot a shark* | Time; keeps one continuous and one simple present verb |
| *Harry the superhero saves the lives of hundreds of thousands of people using magic* → *Harry the superhero saves thousands of lives* | Shorter to read and say; the tense point is unchanged |
| Added: form reminders (+s, +ed, am/is/are + -ing, was/were + -ing) | The pages assume the forms; a 3-second reminder helps weaker students |
| Added: gloss *house husband = a man who stays at home to look after the home and family* | Likely unfamiliar to students |
| Added: *when + short action · while + long action* tip chips | A memorable rule drawn from the textbook's two examples |
| Added: verb groups in the No -ing Zone (feelings, thinking, senses, owning) | Easier to remember than a flat list |
| Snow White speech bubbles used as the hook and answered in Level 4 | Gives students a reason to watch to the end |
